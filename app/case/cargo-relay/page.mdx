## The idea

Every weekday, thousands of people travel the same routes through Lausanne. CarGo Relay is a **C2C parcel-delivery app** that uses this everyday human flow: parcels travel with people who are already going that way, mostly on the busiest routes from Monday to Friday.

I was one of three co-founders, backed by **EPFL Startup Support**.

## Route matching

Travellers share their current location, their usual destination and how they travel. Each mode gets parcels that fit it:

- **Metro riders:** parcels close to their route, near where they start and end
- **Bikes:** more flexible, with wider detours
- **Cars:** the most flexible, and the only option for large parcels

The matching runs on the **Google Maps API**.

## What I built

I led the system design and built the product end to end:

- **React Native** app for senders and travellers
- **FastAPI** backend
- **MariaDB** for the data model

## Result

By the end of 2024, CarGo Relay had **~5,000 registered users** across universities and schools in Lausanne, and **~CHF 50,000 in revenue** from a 10% commission on deliveries.

## What I learned

- **Build on behavior that already exists.** People were already traveling these routes; the product only had to match parcels to them.
- **Design for each mode.** A metro rider and a driver can't take the same parcel, so the matching had to respect how people move.
